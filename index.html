Instagram login first.

ReelPilot now opens Instagram’s official login page before Reels. Your existing on-device Instagram session is retained; Instagram decides whether you need to sign in again.

Install ReelPilot update
  1. Open this page in Safari on your registered iPhone.
  2. Tap Install ReelPilot update and confirm the iOS prompt. Do not delete the existing app.
  3. Open ReelPilot after installation. While parked or as a passenger, continue and sign in directly on Instagram if asked.

What changed

Login and popup handling passed automated fixture tests; live Instagram account behavior on the iPhone still needs confirmation.
