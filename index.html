REGISTERED IPHONE BUILD · 1.0 (4)

Instagram, edge to edge.

The Instagram web surface now extends to the screen edges instead of sitting inside native black top and bottom strips. The small … session menu stays in the safe area.

Install ReelPilot update
  1. Open this page in Safari on your registered iPhone.
  2. Tap Install ReelPilot update and confirm the iOS prompt. Do not delete the existing app. Updating in place retains its Instagram session data.
  3. Open ReelPilot after installation. While parked or as a passenger, continue and sign in directly on Instagram if asked.

What changed

Removed the native black player backing and extended only the web surface beneath device safe areas. WebKit keeps automatic content and scroll-indicator insets, and keyboard avoidance remains enabled. Videos are not stretched or cropped; Instagram’s own controls, security and consent dialogs are not hidden. Black areas that belong to Instagram or a video’s original aspect ratio may still appear.

Build 3’s official Instagram login-first entry and narrowly scoped “Watch this reel in the app” promotion handling are unchanged. No passwords are collected and no session data is cleared.

Passed 42 automated tests, Swift state tests, a signed iPhone archive/export, and package verification. This build has not been visually tested on the physical iPhone. Please check notch/home-indicator controls and the sign-in keyboard while parked.

Steering-wheel test

While parked: play a Reel with sound, open the … menu and choose Enable steering-wheel scrolling. Keep ReelPilot open, then try Next. Re-enable after backgrounding or diagnostics. Actual car event delivery and subsequent playback remain unverified.

Ad hoc update for previously provisioned devices only. No TestFlight. Not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
